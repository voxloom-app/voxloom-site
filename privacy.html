On-device

Privacy

Voxloom transcribes speech on your Mac. Here is exactly what touches the network and what stays local.

TRANSCRIPTION

Speech-to-text runs locally

Audio is transcribed on your Mac with whisper.cpp. Voxloom has no cloud speech-to-text path.

CLEANUP

Optional cleanup stays on-device

Experimental local cleanup uses Qwen3 0.6B to process your transcript on your Mac. No audio or transcript is sent to a cloud model. If cleanup fails its checks, Voxloom uses the uncleaned transcript.

NETWORK

User-started model downloads

Whisper model files come from Hugging Face (ggerganov/whisper.cpp), only after you choose to download them. The optional cleanup model comes from unsloth/Qwen3-0.6B-GGUF on Hugging Face when you choose Download in Settings.

In-app model downloads use pinned revisions and check the exact file size and SHA-256 before installation. There are no silent downloads. Model downloads do not upload your dictation.

STORAGE

What's saved, and where

  • History: raw and final transcript text, cleanup metadata, status, duration, model, timestamp and delivery outcome, saved as unencrypted JSON in history.json in the app's data folder on your Mac. Other processes running as your user, and backups of the folder, can read it.
  • Transcript retention setting: Off (no transcripts kept), 30 days, or until deleted.
  • Word dictionary: dictionary.json. Settings: settings.json. Both are stored locally.
  • Recorded audio isn't saved for later. Saved audio retention is not implemented.
  • Upgrade or unreadable-file backups may contain older transcripts; retention never deletes those backups. See the app privacy documentation for manual deletion details.
CLIPBOARD

Copy fallback and paste

If direct insertion is not possible and Clipboard fallback is enabled, Voxloom can copy the transcript or use a temporary clipboard paste in supported apps. The paste route attempts to restore your previous clipboard unless it has changed. Other apps and clipboard managers may still read the transcript. Turn Clipboard fallback off if that matters to you. Voxloom never types into password fields and never presses Send.
