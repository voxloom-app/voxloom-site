Built for people who type all day.

01 / LOCAL

Whisper, on-device.

Speech-to-text runs on your Mac via whisper.cpp. No audio or transcript leaves it without your explicit action. Models download only when you say so.

02 / ANYWHERE

Types into any field.

Text lands in the field focused when transcription finishes. Unsafe? It can Copy instead.

03 / GESTURES

Hold or latch.

Hold-to-talk, or double-tap to latch for longer thoughts.

04 / HISTORY

Your history. Your choice.

Keep transcripts on your Mac, depending on your retention setting, or turn retention off.

05 / DICTIONARY

Your vocabulary.

Save names and technical terms in a personal word dictionary. Matching words use your saved spelling, correcting case and spacing even with local cleanup off.

06 / GUARDRAILS

Polite by default.

Never inserts into password fields. Never steals focus. Never presses Send. Every result is labeled Inserted, Copied, Failed or Unverified.

07 / CLEANUP

Local cleanup. Experimental.

An optional on-device model fixes punctuation and drops filler words. If cleanup fails its checks, Voxloom uses the uncleaned transcript. Set up local cleanup.
